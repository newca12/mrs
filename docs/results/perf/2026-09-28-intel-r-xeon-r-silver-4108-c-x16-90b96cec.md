`Processed/s` and `Generated/s` are aggregate rates over all workers, so a
multi-worker row is total throughput, not per-core throughput. `Work SHA`
is the digest of the iteration counters: rows sharing a work SHA did
identical work, which is what makes their timings comparable. `Noise` is
the spread of the repeat runs this row is the best of: a difference between
two hosts smaller than their combined noise is not a result.
