# CASC Performance Analysis & Improvement Roadmap

Based on the detailed CASC-30 `run.csv` benchmark results and the architectural gaps documented in `SOTA.md` and `STATUS.md`, the `mrs` prover is hitting specific combinatorial and heuristic walls. Solving 139/1101 (12.6%) is a solid baseline for a custom pure-Rust prover, but targeted fixes to the most glaring failure modes can drastically increase this score.

Here is the analytical breakdown of the recent CASC run and the highest-ROI technical investments to improve it:

## 1. Premature Saturation: SInE Over-Pruning
* **The Data:** In `FNE` and `FEQ`, `mrs` frequently returns `GaveUp` in under 1 second (e.g., `NUM656+4` in 0.038s, `SWW963+1` in 0.033s, `CSR040+3` in 0.113s). 
* **The Diagnosis:** Returning `GaveUp` in milliseconds means the prover completely exhausted its search space (saturation) without finding a proof. For complex FOF problems, this almost certainly means the **Sumo Inference Engine (SInE)** is acting too aggressively. It is filtering out axioms that are actually required for the proof, leaving a trivially satisfiable core that saturates instantly.
* **The Fix:** 
  1. Implement a fallback strategy: If the prover saturates in `< 1` second, automatically restart the given-clause loop with SInE disabled or with a significantly relaxed tolerance.
  2. Tune the genericity thresholds and depth limits in the SInE heuristic.

## 2. The EPU/EPS Anomaly: Naive Grounding is Exploding
* **The Data:** `EPU` (EPR Unsat): 8/100 solved. `EPS` (EPR Sat): 23/100 solved.
* **The Diagnosis:** The `preprocess_epr` stage is highly polarized. When it succeeds, it solves problems instantly (e.g., `LAT260-2` in 0.059s, `PUZ001-3` in 0.036s). When it fails, it hangs until the 120s timeout. This indicates the pre-clause-loop ground instance enumeration is hitting a hard wall, likely generating SAT instances so massive that `varisat` chokes or OOMs.
* **The Fix:** Replace naive ground instantiation with true **InstGen** (instantiation calculus with unification) or rely entirely on the `varisat`-backed **AVATAR** architecture to handle EPR dynamically and lazily without pre-grounding.

## 3. Mid-Run Collapse: Memory / Queue Exhaustion
* **The Data:** In `UEQ` and `ICU`, many problems return `GaveUp` at arbitrary times well before the timeout (e.g., `LAT072-1` at 72s, `SWW348+1` at 90s, `GRP660-10` at 98s).
* **The Diagnosis:** A `GaveUp` that occurs halfway through the time limit usually indicates the prover hit an internal safety limit (e.g., a hard cap on the size of the passive queue) or aborted due to an Out-Of-Memory (OOM) trajectory. The superposition search space is exploding faster than the redundancy elimination can cull it.
* **The Fix:** Implement **Global Subsumption & Orphan Elimination**. Deleting clauses generated by parents that were later subsumed is critical for keeping the `BinaryHeap` passive queue from consuming all available RAM.

## 4. The UEQ Bleed: Missing AC-Unification
* **The Data:** `UEQ` (Unit Equality): 32/300 solved. **Average time: 47.6s.**
* **The Diagnosis:** This is the highest average solve time and the second-largest division. Unit Equality problems consist entirely of equations. Because `mrs` lacks **Associative-Commutative (AC) Unification**, any axiom stating `f(x,y) = f(y,x)` causes a permutation explosion. The prover spends all 47 seconds generating identical symmetric clauses until it times out.
* **The Fix:** Implement AC-Unification. Treating AC properties algorithmically rather than axiomatically will instantly collapse the search space in this division.

## 5. The FEQ/FNE Bottleneck: Imperfect Indexing
* **The Data:** `FEQ` (FOF with Equality): 45/400 (11%). `FNE` (FOF Non-Equational): 30/100 (30%). 
* **The Diagnosis:** `FEQ` is the largest division in CASC. The primary bottleneck here is unification speed. The current `DTree` is an *imperfect* index. This means the index returns many "false positive" unification candidates that must be manually rejected by a costly strict-unification check.
* **The Fix:** Upgrade the `DTree` to a **Perfect Discrimination Tree** or a **Substitution Tree**. In `FEQ`, the active clause set easily reaches hundreds of thousands of clauses; eliminating $O(N)$ false-positive unification checks per given-clause will yield a massive speedup.

---

## Summary of Priorities

To maximize the CASC score, prioritize development in this order:
1. **SInE Fallback:** The easiest fix. Restart on sub-second saturation. Reclaims instant points in FNE/FEQ.
2. **Fix EPU Grounding:** Stop pre-enumerating ground instances in EPR.
3. **Upgrade to Perfect D-Trees:** Speeds up every single division, particularly `FEQ` (the largest pool of points).
4. **Implement Orphan Elimination:** Prevents mid-run memory exhaustion in UEQ/ICU.
5. **Implement AC-Unification:** Unblocks the `UEQ` division and stops permutation timeouts.
