Notes for the organizer
------------------------

mrs is entered in FOF and UEQ only (withdrawn from FNT — no
finite-model-building component).

Both issues flagged in review of the previous submission are fixed:

1. **FOF-to-CNF translation steps are now documented.** The SEU140+2 proof
   above shows the full pipeline for both axioms and the negated conjecture:
   the original `fof(..., axiom/conjecture, ...)` leaf, an explicit
   `negated_conjecture` step (`status(cth)`, single parent of role
   `conjecture`, per the evaluation criteria), `fof_nnf_transformation`
   (`status(thm)`), `skolemisation` (`status(esa)`), and finally
   `cnf_transformation` producing the flat clauses used by the given-clause
   loop. Every clause traces back through this chain to the original problem
   file rather than citing it directly.

2. **The invalid `__def_..._N__` symbol is fixed.** mrs's definitional-CNF
   (Tseitin) transformation, used when a formula has a conjunction nested
   under a disjunction (as in SEU140+2's `t3_xboole_0` lemma), now generates
   names of the form `def_<prefix>_<n>` (e.g. `def_t3_xboole_0_0` above) —
   starting with a lowercase letter, per the TPTP `lower_word`/`functor`
   grammar — instead of the previous `__def_<prefix>_<n>__`, which started
   with an invalid leading underscore.

The BOO001-1 (UEQ) proof is included again for completeness; it is
unaffected by either fix, since UEQ problems are supplied already in clausal
form and never go through the FOF-to-CNF pipeline.
