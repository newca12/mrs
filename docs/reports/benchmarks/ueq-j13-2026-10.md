## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20 and October 21, 2026, two full standalone CASC-J13 UEQ (Unit Equality)
campaigns were evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints on `mtsdev04`:

- **400 problems evaluated** per campaign at the official CASC-J13 wall-clock limit of **180 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Campaign wall clock times were 55,160 s (~15.32 hours)
  on Oct 20 and 55,070 s (~15.30 hours) on Oct 21.
- **120 refutations generated on Oct 20 (30.00%) and 119 on Oct 21 (29.75%)**:
  118 refutations solved on Oct 20 re-solved on Oct 21. In addition, `REL027-3` (which timed out
  at 178.8 s on Oct 20) solved in 84.78 s on Oct 21 and certified as `VerifiedGood`.
  Two borderline problems near the 180 s limit (`ALG240-1` at 178.3 s and `NUN133-1` at 159.5 s)
  timed out on Oct 21.
- **Native Production Confirmation of Leaf Symmetry Matching (`b1033d3`)**:
  - In the Oct 20 campaign, 6 refutations were rejected as `VerifiedBad` due to flipped equality
    orientation in cited axiom leaves (`GRP178-1`, `GRP183-3`, `GRP186-1`, `REL026-3`, `REL041-2`,
    `REL045-2`), yielding 112 `VerifiedGood`.
  - In the Oct 21 campaign, running with the fixed kernel (`b1033d3`), **all six problems certified
    natively as `VerifiedGood` in live production**, achieving **117 `VerifiedGood` (98.32%)**
    with **0 `VerifiedBad` observed**.
- **Residual AC replay barrier resolved**: The 2 proofs returning `Unknown` in the original audit
  (`RNG009-5` and `RNG009-7`) were caused by incomplete contiguous sub-chain operand placement and
  missing associative leaf absorption in `ac_subset_superposition_replay` and `for_each_ac_unifier`.
  With these repairs and background AC inclusion in `mrs-proof-kernel`, both proofs certify
  strictly, bringing the audited yield to **119 of 119 `VerifiedGood` (100.0%)**!
- **Speed & Scale**: Median solve time across all refutations was **13.707 s** on Oct 20 and
  **14.078 s** on Oct 21; 21 solved in under 1 second, and 55 in under 10 seconds. Maximum observed RSS
  was 57,732 MB (`ROB015-10`, Timeout) in both runs.

## 4. Analysis of Audit Failures

### 4.1 Leaf Orientation Mismatches (`VerifiedBad`)

Six proofs were rejected because the prover serialized an oriented equation
as an axiom leaf where the source problem file defined it in the opposite orientation:

| Problem | Step | Source Axiom | Proof Leaf Formula | Source Axiom Formula |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **`GRP178-1`** | `c13` | `monotony_glb2` | `multiply(glb(Y,X),Z) = glb(...)` | `glb(...) = multiply(glb(Y,X),Z)` |
| **`GRP183-3`** | `c9` | `monotony_glb2` | `multiply(glb(Y,X),Z) = glb(...)` | `glb(...) = multiply(glb(Y,X),Z)` |
| **`GRP186-1`** | `c9` | `monotony_glb2` | `multiply(glb(Y,X),Z) = glb(...)` | `glb(...) = multiply(glb(Y,X),Z)` |
| **`REL026-3`** | `c17` | `dedekind_law_14` | `join(...) = composition(...)` | `composition(...) = join(...)` |
| **`REL041-2`** | `c17` | `dedekind_law_14` | `join(...) = composition(...)` | `composition(...) = join(...)` |
| **`REL045-2`** | `c16` | `dedekind_law_14` | `join(...) = composition(...)` | `composition(...) = join(...)` |

In all six cases, the underlying mathematical inference is sound; the failure
arises because the strict proof kernel checks cited axiom leaves against problem
formulas modulo alpha-equivalence and AC connectives, but does not allow symmetric
reorientation of equality literals at problem-leaf matching.

### 4.3 Post-Campaign Kernel Fix (`b1033d3`) & Live Production Re-confirmation

Following the October 20 campaign, commit `b1033d3` (`fix(proof-kernel): match CNF leaves
modulo equality symmetry`) enhanced `mrs-proof-kernel` to compare CNF leaves
modulo equality orientation symmetry with backtracking over variable bijections.

In the October 21 campaign (`campaign-casc-j13-04-ueq-CASCHJ1-20261021`), this fix was
evaluated in full production from scratch:
- **All six previously rejected problems certified natively as `VerifiedGood`**
  (`GRP178-1`, `GRP183-3`, `GRP186-1`, `REL026-3`, `REL041-2`, `REL045-2`).
- **`VerifiedBad` was exactly 0**: Zero false rejections and zero unsound deductions.
- **`REL027-3` solved in 84.78 s** and certified strictly as `VerifiedGood` (previously timed out at 178.8 s on Oct 20).
- **Two borderline problems near 180 s** timed out on Oct 21: `ALG240-1` (timed out at 178.7 s vs 178.3 s solve on Oct 20)
  and `NUN133-1` (timed out at 179.1 s vs 159.5 s solve on Oct 20).
- Net strictly certified score: **117 of 119 `VerifiedGood` (98.32%)** originally, and
  **119 of 119 `VerifiedGood` (100.0%)** after completing associative sub-chain and segment replay
  in `mrs-proof-kernel` (`RNG009-5` and `RNG009-7` resolved).
