## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20, 2026, a full standalone CASC-J13 UEQ (Unit Equality) campaign
was evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints:

- **400 problems evaluated** at the official CASC-J13 wall-clock limit of **180 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Total campaign wall clock time was 55,160 s
  (~15.32 hours).
- **120 refutations generated (`Unsatisfiable`)**: 30.00% raw solve rate. The
  remaining outcomes were 266 `Timeout` (66.50%) and 14 `GaveUp` (3.50%), with
  zero process errors or crashes.
- **Strict self-certification: 112 `VerifiedGood` (93.33% of refutations)**,
  6 `VerifiedBad` (5.00%), 2 `Unknown` (1.67%), 0 checker timeouts, and 0
  omitted proofs.
- **Diagnosis of `VerifiedBad` rejections & Kernel Fix (`b1033d3`)**: All six
  `VerifiedBad` results are due to syntactic orientation flips in cited axiom
  leaves rather than unsound deductive steps:
  - `GRP178-1`, `GRP183-3`, and `GRP186-1`: leaf equality flipped against
    source axiom `monotony_glb2` (`multiply(glb(Y,X), Z) = ...` vs. `glb(...) = multiply(...)`).
  - `REL026-3`, `REL041-2`, and `REL045-2`: leaf equality flipped against
    source axiom `dedekind_law_14` (`join(...) = composition(...)` vs. `composition(...) = join(...)`).
  In both families, term ordering (KBO/LPO) canonicalized the equation upon input
  ingestion, and the proof serializer emitted the oriented equation as the axiom leaf.
  Commit `b1033d3` (`fix(proof-kernel): match CNF leaves modulo equality symmetry`)
  resolved this limitation in the kernel; re-verifying the six archived proofs
  certifies all six as **`VerifiedGood`**, bringing the re-audited strictly verified
  score to **118 of 120 (98.33%)** with **0 `VerifiedBad`**.
- **Residual AC replay barrier**: The 2 residual `Unknown` proofs (`RNG009-5`
  and `RNG009-7`) return `Unknown` due to incomplete AC superposition rewrite replay.
- **Speed & Scale**: Median solve time across all refutations was **13.707 s**;
  21 solved in under 1 second, and 55 in under 10 seconds. Maximum observed RSS
  was 57,732 MB (`ROB015-10`, Timeout).

## 4. Analysis of Audit Failures

### 4.1 Leaf Orientation Mismatches (`VerifiedBad`)

Six proofs were rejected because the prover serialized an oriented equation
as an axiom leaf where the source problem file defined it in the opposite orientation:

| Problem | Step | Source Axiom | Proof Leaf Formula | Source Axiom Formula |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **`GRP178-1`** | `c13` | `monotony_glb2` | `multiply(glb(Y,X),Z) = glb(...)` | `glb(...) = multiply(glb(Y,X),Z)` |
| **`GRP183-3`** | `c9` | `monotony_glb2` | `multiply(glb(Y,X),Z) = glb(...)` | `glb(...) = multiply(glb(Y,X),Z)` |
| **`GRP186-1`** | `c9` | `monotony_glb2` | `multiply(glb(Y,X),Z) = glb(...)` | `glb(...) = multiply(glb(Y,X),Z)` |
| **`REL026-3`** | `c17` | `dedekind_law_14` | `join(...) = composition(...)` | `composition(...) = join(...)` |
| **`REL041-2`** | `c17` | `dedekind_law_14` | `join(...) = composition(...)` | `composition(...) = join(...)` |
| **`REL045-2`** | `c16` | `dedekind_law_14` | `join(...) = composition(...)` | `composition(...) = join(...)` |

In all six cases, the underlying mathematical inference is sound; the failure
arises because the strict proof kernel checks cited axiom leaves against problem
formulas modulo alpha-equivalence and AC connectives, but does not allow symmetric
reorientation of equality literals at problem-leaf matching.
