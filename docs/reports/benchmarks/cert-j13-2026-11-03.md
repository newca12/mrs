### Strict proof audit

The archived audit used `checks=[strict]`. All **253/253 applicable refutations
were `VerifiedGood`**; there were 0 `VerifiedBad`, 0 `Unknown`, 0 verifier
timeouts, and 0 proof omissions. The other 547 rows were `N/A: Incomplete`
because search produced no refutation. No result in this campaign had a missing
or omitted proof.

The audit also rechecked `LAT142-1` as `VerifiedGood`, exercising the AC
operand-absorption replay fix in a fresh full-run proof. `REL045-2` again emitted
a complete proof (159 nodes, 26,161 bytes) and passed strict verification,
reconfirming the proof-extraction fix tracked in UI-12.

## Comparison and interpretation

| Division | October 30 | November 3 | Difference |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| FEQ | 70/300 | **72/300** | +2 |
| FNE | 38/100 | **38/100** | 0 |
| UEQ | 141/400 | **143/400** | +2 |
| **Total raw refutations** | **249/800** | **253/800** | **+4** |
| **Strict `VerifiedGood`** | **249/249** after re-audit | **253/253** | **+4** |

The October 30 archived audit initially recorded 248 `VerifiedGood` and one
`Unknown` (`LAT142-1`); the subsequent strict re-audit with the AC matcher fix
raised that result to 249/249. The correct comparison is therefore +4 strict
refutations over the re-audited October 30 result, not +5 over its original
audit summary. No previously refuted problem was lost in the November 3 run.

Both campaigns used `mtsdev02`, eight workers, `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`,
`jobs=1`, auto schedule, and the same official time limits. They used different
dirty producer builds, however, and this is one run per build rather than a
randomized paired experiment. The +4 is a descriptive archive-to-archive delta;
it does not establish a causal code improvement or a stable coverage gain. The
November 3 result is the latest full-campaign observation, not a standardized
baseline or an official CASC score.
