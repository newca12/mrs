# CASC-J13 certification campaign (2026-10-28)

> Supplemental archived result, not a current coverage baseline. The campaign
> ran a stale, dirty producer binary on a host with more than eight physical
> cores, and 41 of its 232 raw refutations had no derivation to certify. Keep
> raw SZS results, strict re-audit results, and later targeted reproductions
> separate.

## Results

The campaign covered the full 800-problem CASC-J13 FEQ, FNE, and UEQ corpus.
The archived `run.csv` reports:

| Division | Problems | Raw refutations | `GaveUp` | `Timeout` |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FEQ | 300 | 74 `Theorem` | 57 | 169 |
| FNE | 100 | 39 `Theorem` | 14 | 47 |
| UEQ | 400 | 119 `Unsatisfiable` | 17 | 264 |
| **Total** | **800** | **232** | **88** | **480** |

Every process exited with status 0; no `Error` or `ResourceOut` result was
recorded. The sum of per-problem `actual_wall_time_s` is 108,305.8 seconds
(30.08 hours, `jobs=1`). Median solve times were 1.643 s for FEQ, 2.906 s for
FNE, and 12.511 s for UEQ. These are descriptive statistics for this one run,
not estimates of stable performance.
The maximum recorded per-row RSS was 76,732 MB and maximum `VmPeak` was
89,198 MB (both on `NLP261+1`); these metrics are distinct, and no row reported
a resource-limit result.

proof:

|:---|---:|:---|:---|
| FEQ | 74 | 67 `VerifiedGood`, 1 `VerifiedBad`, 5 `no_proof`, 1 `proof_omitted` | **68 `VerifiedGood`**, 0 `VerifiedBad`, 5 `no_proof`, 1 `proof_omitted` |
| FNE | 39 | 39 `VerifiedGood` | **39 `VerifiedGood`** |

The current re-audit moved `GEO526+1` from `VerifiedBad` to `VerifiedGood`:
commit `5fa668b` corrected a strict-kernel condensation false rejection, and the
retained proof now passes. This is a checker re-audit, not a newly generated
solve. The other 41 `no_proof` rows still have no derivation that can be
certified, and `SWV406+1` remains proof-omitted because its 134,538,096-byte,
515,090-node proof exceeded the configured output limit. Therefore **190/232
raw refutations are strictly certified in the current re-audit**; do not report
232 as certified coverage.

`casc` fixes the worker count at eight but does not pin the process to eight
physical cores. This host had 16 physical cores, so the run is not an eight-core
simulation. The host also had less than the 128 GiB CASC memory allowance; the
per-run logs report an effective budget of 108,816 MB. These constraints,
alongside the single dirty build, make this supplemental evidence rather than a
standardized CASC comparison.

Most importantly, `run.log` warned that the recorded producer binary was older
than the sources. The recorded commit predates `c619394`, which fixed AC
inference parent handling in TSTP serialization. This explains why the
campaign's proof-output behavior must not be read as behavior of the current
source tree.

## Proof-output observations

The 41 `no_proof` rows divide into two visible output shapes:

* **40 empty SZS proof envelopes** (5 FEQ and 35 UEQ): there are no `cnf(...)` or
  `fof(...)` derivation steps between the SZS proof markers. This is consistent
  with the known old serializer failure: before `c619394`, a binary AC
  inference's extra cited AC axioms failed the exact-two-parent check, and
  `format_tstp` failed closed to an empty string. The run metadata confirms the
  producer executable was stale. The archive does not retain the in-memory DAG,
  however, so this mechanism is not independently established for each of the
  40 rows; they remain uncertified regardless.
* **One header-only proof**, `REL045-2`: its stdout contains only a `% Proof :`
  header, and telemetry records `proof_nodes=0 proof_bytes=72
  proof_emitted=true`. This is the missing-dependency proof-extraction defect
  documented in [UI-12](../../policies/unresolved-issues.md#ui-12--j13-rel045-2-refutation-has-no-proof-root-and-misleading-proof_emitted-telemetry).

The 40 empty-envelope problems are:

* FEQ: `KLE170+1.002`, `LCL901+1`, `NUM835+1`, `NUM840+1`, `NUM853+2`.
* UEQ: `GRP167-3`, `GRP178-1`, `GRP183-3`, `GRP186-1`, `KLE044-10`,
  `KLE084-10`, `KLE145-10`, `KLE152-10`, `KLE169-10`, `LAT017-1`, `LAT018-1`,
  `LAT044-1`, `LAT157-1`, `LAT171-1`, `LAT222-10`, `LAT241-10`, `LCL160-1`,
  `LCL890-10`, `LCL897-10`, `MVA009-1`, `NUN133-1`, `REL010-2`, `REL012-1`,
  `REL012-2`, `REL019-1`, `REL024-1`, `REL031-1`, `REL042-1`, `REL042-2`,
  `REL043-2`, `REL044-2`, `REL050-1`, `REL050-3`, `RNG009-5`, `RNG009-7`.

Current-source spot checks show that at least two of these problems now produce
certifiable proofs: `NUM853+2` produced a 63-node proof under Strategy 14, and
`REL024-1` produced a 117-node proof under `casc_ueq`; both passed strict replay.
These targeted runs do **not** retroactively certify the archived campaign rows
or establish that all 40 empty envelopes are repaired. A fresh full campaign is
needed for current whole-division coverage. The separate current-source
`REL045-2` reproduction is recorded under UI-12.

## Re-audit details

`31b81f32e312f2fb9f142d79a95d163056356b29`, `--strict-time 120`, and four audit
jobs. The archived audit identity records checker SHA-256
`22a48a66590d7b642126f7565c35047b32cea42e8da5eeb62561c0bca7299273` and
`mrs-proover` SHA-256
`ac4686ed37932740e890557448e814284e48e3408ff1ba75da2cbeceb356b24b`. The
current audit executable SHA-256 was
`6023ef424d835506e69326f4a8afc55117c1a98be71b4827431f73ce4c01f575`; the
`mrs-proover` SHA-256 was
`2ae2253ad3488483f7e04778b8e947f62a0d20f57642be7d0ba016b8cb83fde4`. To repeat

