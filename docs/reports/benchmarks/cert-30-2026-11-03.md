# CASC-30 campaign interrupted during ICU (2026-11-03)

> This is a partial campaign archive, not a full CASC-30 result. The job
> manifest contains 1,101 jobs (including all 101 ICU problems), but `run.csv`
> contains only 1,032 rows: all 1,000 non-ICU jobs and the first 32 ICU jobs.
> The saved run stops after `CSI007+1`, before `CSI008+1`; 69 ICU jobs have no
> result row. The strict audit below covers only the saved rows.

## Completeness and interruption evidence

The campaign began at `20261103_111821` on `mtsdev01`. Its `run.log` ends at
`1032/1101 completed`; `.jobs` still contains 1,101 entries, `run.csv.lock`
remains, and the normal final `[casc] Done` line is absent. `run.csv` has 1,033
lines including its header. The last saved problem is `ICU/CSI007+1`; the next
manifest entry is `ICU/CSI008+1`. There is no archived stdout/stderr or CSV row
for `CSI008+1` or the 68 ICU entries after it. The last `run.csv` write is
timestamped 2026-11-05 08:34:01 +0100; use that as the initial window to
correlate with remote boot/kernel logs.

The operator reports that the remote host rebooted during ICU. These artifacts
show an abrupt stop after `CSI007+1`, with `CSI008+1` next in the manifest;
they do not establish whether that solver had started or prove the reboot cause.
`CSI008+1` is a strong point to investigate: the earlier ICU
report records this 2,004,866-axiom problem being killed by the OS OOM killer
on a smaller-memory host. A host journal, persistent kernel log, or BMC event
log is needed to distinguish memory pressure from a power/reset event or an
external process termination. The local development environment could not
resolve `mtsdev01` over SSH, so those remote logs were not available during this
review. See the [remote reboot investigation guide](../../guides/remote-only-work.md#investigating-a-campaign-host-reboot).

The saved `run.log` records `casc.sh` progress but does not identify the outer
invocation. If `certification_campaign.sh` was used, its audit phase runs only
after the search returns, so a reboot during search prevents it from creating
`certification/`. If `casc.sh` was launched directly, it never creates that
directory. This review has now audited the saved rows separately.

The same artifact signature is present in the October 30 run: 1,101 manifest
jobs, 1,032 result rows, and the same cutoff immediately before `CSI008+1`.
Its last `run.csv` write is timestamped 2026-11-01 16:11:02 +0100.
The earlier report called that run “full”; that description is corrected in
the [October 30 report](cert-30-2026-10-30.md). Neither attempt is a complete
101-problem ICU campaign.

## Comparison with October 30 and resource context

A current-kernel re-audit of the saved October 30 proofs yields 308 strictly
verified refutations plus 40 certified EPS models (348 total). This November 3
archive yields 310 plus 40 (350 total): three newly certified problems
(`SWV459+1`, `LAT230-10`, `REL036-2`) and one prior certified problem not
reproduced (`LCL416-10`), for a net +2. This is a single archive-to-archive
observation from dirty producer trees, not a paired or causal comparison. The
raw solve count is 357 versus 356; `GEO559+1` was a raw theorem on October 30
but its proof was omitted, and it timed out in this run.

The producer metadata records commit `57c73039d5a0f1809dc1a3c08af7a6838e3fe794`
on `main`, `git_dirty=true`, binary SHA-256
`06567e46e9fa02f0d42d812acc6f1454e35e6d04d26d6eefe9fdc1e1765568f4`, and Rust
1.99.1. The host reported eight physical cores and 96,350 MB RAM. The `casc`
profile reported `effective_mem_mb=81897` against its 128-GiB budget, with
`mem_unrepresentable=1`. The saved rows sum to 162,841.5 seconds (45.23 hours)
of actual solver wall time. The highest recorded RSS is 76,331 MB on
`FNE/NLP261+1`; no memory-resource exit was recorded before the archive stops.

Do not quote 350 as a full CASC-30 campaign score: it is the strictly certified
yield among the rows that survived in this interrupted archive. The full ICU
