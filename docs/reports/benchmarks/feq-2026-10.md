## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20, 2026, a full standalone CASC-30 FEQ (First-Order with Equality)
campaign was evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints:

- **400 problems evaluated** at the official CASC-30 wall-clock limit of **240 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Total campaign wall clock time was 73,664 s
  (~20.46 hours).
- **108 refutations generated (`Theorem`)**: 27.0% raw solve rate. The remaining
  outcomes were 249 `Timeout` (62.25%) and 43 `GaveUp` (10.75%).
- **Strict self-certification: 103 of 108 `VerifiedGood` (95.37%)**, with **0
  `VerifiedBad` (0.0% unsoundness)**, 2 `Unknown`, 1 verifier `Timeout`, and 2
  `Proof Omitted`.
- **Zero silent kills / 100% telemetry completion**: All 400 problems terminated
  cleanly with `% SZS detail` telemetry. The quadratic SOS pre-flight bug
  documented in `docs/reports/benchmarks/feq-silent-kills-investigation.md` is
  confirmed resolved in full production: previously hung problems (`BIO005+1`,
  `BIO006+1`, `CSR047+5`, `CSR052+4`, `HWV090+1`, `HWV128+1`, `ITP015+4`,
  `SWX070+1`) terminated cleanly, and **both `BIO005+1` and `BIO006+1` solved as
  strictly verified theorems** (~157 s).
- **Resolution of previous `VerifiedBad` proofs**: The two refutations that were
  rejected as `VerifiedBad` in the 2026-10-16 campaign—`KLE071+1` (inconsistent
  symbol arity) and `SWV486+2` (AC superposition conclusion validation)—both now
  certify strictly as **`VerifiedGood`**.
- **Proof size ceiling**: `SET017+1` (49.3 MB proof, 206k nodes) and `SET072+1`
  (45.2 MB proof, 189k nodes) derived refutations in 232 s and 200 s, but
  withheld proof text (`proof_emitted=false`) because they exceeded the default
  32 MB (`--proof-bytes-limit`) ceiling.

## 4. Solved Problem Analysis & Key Observations

### 4.1 Non-VerifiedGood Refutations

All five non-VerifiedGood refutations are accounted for:

1. **`ALG049+1` — Verifier Timeout (125.07 s)**
   - Search: Solved in 5.41 s (8,198 proof nodes, 1.46 MB proof).
   - Verifier: Exceeded the 120 s strict audit per-proof limit. This is a
     kernel checking speed bottleneck on deep definition expansions, not a
     search or soundness failure.
2. **`KLE132+1` — Unknown (AC Superposition Replay)**
   - Search: Solved in 11.63 s (14,690 proof nodes, 2.77 MB proof).
   - Strict Verifier: Returned `Unknown` in 1.31 s at node `c103782`:
     `ac_superposition replay is incomplete: superposition conclusion is not a valid rewrite`.
3. **`LCL894+1` — Unknown (AC Superposition Replay)**
   - Search: Solved in 91.02 s (45,768 proof nodes, 10.15 MB proof).
   - Strict Verifier: Returned `Unknown` in 3.53 s at node `c265864`:
     `ac_superposition replay is incomplete: superposition conclusion is not a valid rewrite`.
4. **`SET017+1` — Proof Omitted (49.3 MB)**
   - Search: Solved in 232.12 s (206,688 nodes, 49,309,930 bytes).
   - Emitted `proof_emitted=false` because proof size exceeded the 32 MB
     safety threshold.
5. **`SET072+1` — Proof Omitted (45.2 MB)**
   - Search: Solved in 200.38 s (189,522 nodes, 45,221,542 bytes).
   - Emitted `proof_emitted=false` exceeding the 32 MB safety threshold.

### 4.2 Recovered Proofs (Resolution of Previous Deficiencies)

- **`KLE071+1`**: Rejected as `VerifiedBad` in the 2026-10-16 campaign due to
  `inconsistent symbol signature: symbol 1 used with arities 2 and 1`. Re-checked
  in this campaign and certified cleanly as **`VerifiedGood` (0.18 s)**.
- **`SWV486+2`**: Rejected as `VerifiedBad` in the 2026-10-16 campaign due to
  superposition conclusion validation failure. In this campaign, certified as
  **`VerifiedGood` (5.76 s)** on a 39,046-node proof.
- **`BIO005+1` & `BIO006+1`**: Previously killed silently at 320 s due to the
  quadratic SOS pre-flight check. In this campaign, both solved in 157 s wall time
  and certified as **`VerifiedGood`** (2.58 s and 2.52 s kernel verification).

## 8. Conclusions & Next Steps

1. **Strict Soundness Baseline**: FEQ now has a strictly validated baseline of
   **103 `VerifiedGood`** solves with **zero unsoundness (`VerifiedBad = 0`)**.
2. **AC Superposition Replay**: The remaining kernel replay incompleteness is
   confined to 2 problems (`KLE132+1`, `LCL894+1`). Resolving AC replay on these
   deep rewriting chains will bring them into `VerifiedGood`.
3. **Proof Size Headroom**: Increasing `--proof-bytes-limit` from 32 MB to 64 MB
   in production benchmarks would allow `SET017+1` (49.3 MB) and `SET072+1`
   (45.2 MB) to emit their proofs and be strictly audited.
4. **Verifier Wall-Clock Scaling**: `ALG049+1` derives a proof in 5.4 s, but
   the checker takes 125 s to verify its 8.2k nodes. Optimizing the kernel's
   deep inference replay speed would resolve this verifier timeout within the
   120 s limit.
