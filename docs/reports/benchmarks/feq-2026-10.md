## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20 and October 21, 2026, two full standalone CASC-30 FEQ (First-Order with Equality)
campaigns were evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints:

- **400 problems evaluated** per campaign at the official CASC-30 wall-clock limit of **240 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Campaign wall clock times were 73,664 s (~20.46 hours)
  on Oct 20 and 73,758 s (~20.49 hours) on Oct 21.
- **108 refutations generated (`Theorem`) in both campaigns**: exactly 27.0% raw solve rate.
  Oct 20 had 249 `Timeout` and 43 `GaveUp`; Oct 21 had 244 `Timeout` and 48 `GaveUp`.
- **Identical strict self-certification in both campaigns: 103 of 108 `VerifiedGood` (95.37%)**,
  with **0 `VerifiedBad` (0.0% unsoundness)**, exactly 2 `Unknown`, 1 verifier `Timeout`, and 2
  `Proof Omitted` across both runs.
- **Flawless Soundness Replicability**: Zero unsound proofs across 216 total evaluated refutations.
  The two historical refutations rejected in the 2026-10-16 campaign—`KLE071+1` (inconsistent
  symbol arity) and `SWV486+2` (AC superposition conclusion validation)—certify strictly as
  **`VerifiedGood`** in both runs.
- **Replicate Stability & Intermittent Solves**: 107 of 108 solved problems are identical across
  the two runs. Exactly one borderline problem pair swapped due to portfolio search thread
  racing near the time ceiling: `SWV458+1` solved in 28.4 s on Oct 20 but timed out on Oct 21;
  `SWV457+1` timed out on Oct 20 but solved in 153.4 s on Oct 21, strictly verifying as `VerifiedGood`.
- **Zero silent kills / 100% telemetry completion**: All 400 problems terminated cleanly with
  `% SZS detail` telemetry in both campaigns. The quadratic SOS pre-flight bug (UI-10 / UI-2)
  is conclusively confirmed resolved in full production across both consecutive runs on 96 GB hosts;
  previously hung problems (`BIO005+1`, `BIO006+1`, `CSR047+5`, `CSR052+4`, `HWV090+1`, `HWV128+1`,
  `ITP015+4`, `SWX070+1`) terminated cleanly, and `BIO005+1` and `BIO006+1` solved and strictly
  certified in both campaigns.
- **Proof size ceiling**: `SET017+1` and `SET072+1` derived refutations in both runs but withheld proof
  text (`proof_emitted=false`) because proof sizes (45–49 MB on Oct 20; ~38 MB on Oct 21) exceeded the
  default 32 MB (`--proof-bytes-limit`) ceiling.

## 4. Solved Problem Analysis & Key Observations

### 4.1 Non-VerifiedGood Refutations

All five non-VerifiedGood refutations are accounted for and reproduce identically across both campaigns:

1. **`ALG049+1` — Verifier Timeout (125.07 s on Oct 20; 125.14 s on Oct 21)**
   - Search: Solved in 5.41 s on Oct 20 / 5.28 s on Oct 21 (8,198 proof nodes, 1.46 MB proof).
   - Verifier: Exceeded the 120 s strict audit per-proof limit in both runs. This is a
     kernel checking speed bottleneck on deep definition expansions, not a
     search or soundness failure.
2. **`KLE132+1` — Unknown (AC Superposition Replay)**
   - Search: Solved in 11.63 s on Oct 20 / 11.58 s on Oct 21 (~14.7k proof nodes, ~2.8 MB proof).
   - Strict Verifier: Returned `Unknown` in 1.31 s at node `c103782` on Oct 20, and in 1.27 s
     at node `c107826` on Oct 21:
     `ac_superposition replay is incomplete: superposition conclusion is not a valid rewrite`.
3. **`LCL894+1` — Unknown (AC Superposition Replay)**
   - Search: Solved in 91.02 s on Oct 20 / 89.44 s on Oct 21 (~45.8k proof nodes, ~10.2 MB proof).
   - Strict Verifier: Returned `Unknown` in 3.53 s at node `c265864` on Oct 20, and in 3.43 s
     at node `c270449` on Oct 21:
     `ac_superposition replay is incomplete: superposition conclusion is not a valid rewrite`.
4. **`SET017+1` — Proof Omitted (49.3 MB on Oct 20; 38.2 MB on Oct 21)**
   - Search: Solved in 232.12 s (206k nodes) on Oct 20, and 209.68 s (160k nodes) on Oct 21.
   - Emitted `proof_emitted=false` in both runs because proof size exceeded the 32 MB
     safety threshold.
5. **`SET072+1` — Proof Omitted (45.2 MB on Oct 20; 38.1 MB on Oct 21)**
   - Search: Solved in 200.38 s (189k nodes) on Oct 20, and 198.81 s (158k nodes) on Oct 21.
   - Emitted `proof_emitted=false` exceeding the 32 MB safety threshold in both runs.

### 4.2 Recovered Proofs (Resolution of Previous Deficiencies)

- **`KLE071+1`**: Rejected as `VerifiedBad` in the 2026-10-16 campaign due to
  `inconsistent symbol signature: symbol 1 used with arities 2 and 1`. Re-checked
  in both campaigns and certified cleanly as **`VerifiedGood`** (0.18 s and 0.17 s).
- **`SWV486+2`**: Rejected as `VerifiedBad` in the 2026-10-16 campaign due to
  superposition conclusion validation failure. In both campaigns, certified as
  **`VerifiedGood`** (5.76 s and 5.82 s) on ~39k-node proofs.
- **`BIO005+1` & `BIO006+1`**: Previously killed silently at 320 s due to the
  quadratic SOS pre-flight check. In both campaigns, both solved cleanly in ~157 s wall time
  and certified as **`VerifiedGood`** (~2.5 s kernel verification).

### 4.3 Replicate Stability & Intermittency Analysis

Comparing the Oct 20 and Oct 21 standalone runs provides empirical evidence of multi-worker
portfolio search stability on CASC-30 FEQ:

- **107 of 108 solves are bit-for-bit problem-identical** between the two runs.
- **Exactly 1 borderline problem pair swapped**:
  - `SWV458+1`: Solved in 28.38 s on Oct 20 (`VerifiedGood`, 75 nodes), but timed out at 239.39 s on Oct 21.
  - `SWV457+1`: Timed out at 239.53 s on Oct 20, but solved in 153.37 s on Oct 21 (`VerifiedGood`, 95,867 nodes).
  In both problems, the winning thread was strategy slot 7 (`7:4`), competing against 7 sibling
  search threads on 8 cores. The race condition near the timeout ceiling accounts for the shift,
  maintaining identical net counts (108 Theorems, 103 `VerifiedGood`).
- **GaveUp / Timeout boundary jitter**: 15 unsolved problems shifted between `Timeout` and `GaveUp`
  (e.g., `GEO137+1`, `HAL001+1`, `NUM785+4`), reflecting slight differences in LRS queue pruning
  rates under concurrency near 240 s.

## 8. Conclusions & Next Steps

1. **Replicated Strict Soundness Baseline**: FEQ possesses a firmly verified baseline of
   **103 `VerifiedGood`** solves with **zero unsoundness (`VerifiedBad = 0`)**, reproducible
   across independent runs on canonical 8-core CASC hardware.
2. **Stable Portfolio Solve Capacity**: The portfolio solves exactly 108 problems in both
   replicates with 99% problem overlap (107 identical solves), demonstrating high stability
   with only minor thread racing at the time horizon (`SWV457+1` / `SWV458+1`).
3. **Confirmed Production Resolution of Silent Kills**: UI-10 / UI-2 are fully resolved;
   all 400 problems completed with full telemetry on 96 GB hosts in both consecutive campaigns.
4. **AC Superposition Replay**: The remaining kernel replay incompleteness is strictly
   confined to 2 problems (`KLE132+1`, `LCL894+1`), identical in both runs.
5. **Proof Size Headroom**: Increasing `--proof-bytes-limit` from 32 MB to 64 MB
   in production benchmarks would allow `SET017+1` and `SET072+1` (~38–49 MB) to emit
   their proofs and be strictly audited.
6. **Verifier Wall-Clock Scaling**: `ALG049+1` derives a proof in ~5.3 s, but the checker
   takes ~125 s to verify its 8.2k nodes. Optimizing kernel deep inference replay would resolve
   this verifier timeout within the 120 s limit.
