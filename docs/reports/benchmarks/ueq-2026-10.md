## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20 and October 21, 2026, two full standalone CASC-30 UEQ (Unit Equality)
campaigns were evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints on `mtsdev02`:

- **300 problems evaluated** per campaign at the official CASC-30 wall-clock limit of **240 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Campaign wall clock times were 48,578 s (~13.49 hours)
  on Oct 20 and 48,656 s (~13.52 hours) on Oct 21.
- **124 refutations generated on Oct 20 (41.33%) and 123 on Oct 21 (41.00%)**:
  123 of 124 refutations solved on Oct 20 re-solved identically on Oct 21. Only `REL034-2`
  (which solved in 206.0 s on Oct 20) timed out near the 240 s horizon on Oct 21 (238.8 s).
- **Strict self-certification**:
  - Oct 20: **120 of 124 `VerifiedGood` (96.77%)**, with **0 `VerifiedBad` (0.0% unsoundness)**,
    4 `Unknown`, 0 checker timeouts, and 0 withheld proofs.
  - Oct 21: **119 of 123 `VerifiedGood` (96.75%)**, with **0 `VerifiedBad` (0.0% unsoundness)**,
    4 `Unknown`, 0 checker timeouts, and 0 withheld proofs.
  - Across all 247 refutations across both runs, **zero unsound deductions (`VerifiedBad = 0`)** were detected.
- **Soundness resolution confirmed across both replicates**: The previous CASC-mode run
  (`campaign-casc30-ueq-feq-W8CASCHJ1-20261016`) had 5 proofs rejected as `VerifiedBad` and
  1 refutation without a proof root. In both October standalone campaigns, all soundness bugs
  are confirmed resolved:
  - `MVA008-1` and `MVA009-1` (axiom leaf matching): strictly **`VerifiedGood`** in both runs.
  - `REL034-2` (symbol arity conflict in proof): strictly **`VerifiedGood`** on Oct 20 (timed out on Oct 21).
  - `REL045-2` (missing refutation root): strictly **`VerifiedGood`** in both runs (9.7 s / 9.6 s).
  - `RNG009-5` and `RNG009-7` (incomplete AC rewriting): fail closed as **`Unknown`** in both runs.
- **Residual gaps**: Exactly the same 4 proofs return `Unknown` in both runs (`GRP179-2`, `REL027-3`,
  `RNG009-5`, `RNG009-7`), all due to incomplete AC superposition rewrite replay.

## 4. Detailed Solved Problem Telemetry & Key Observations

### 4.1 Resolution of Prior `VerifiedBad` Deficiencies

In the previous CASC-mode campaign (`campaign-casc30-ueq-feq-W8CASCHJ1-20261016`),
five proofs failed strict replay with `VerifiedBad` and one had a malformed root.
In this campaign:

1. **`MVA008-1` & `MVA009-1`** (Axiom Leaf Matching):
   - Previously failed because cited axiom leaves did not match `residual_b` /
     `residual_c` under syntactic leaf checking.
   - Now certify strictly as **`VerifiedGood`** (108.5 s and 27.0 s wall time).
2. **`REL034-2`** (Symbol Arity Inconsistency):
   - Previously rejected due to signature conflicts across clauses.
   - Now certifies strictly as **`VerifiedGood`** (206.0 s wall time).
3. **`REL045-2`** (Missing Proof Root):
   - Previously generated a refutation but omitted the `$false` root node.
   - Now emits a complete derivation and certifies as **`VerifiedGood`** (9.7 s).
4. **`RNG009-5` & `RNG009-7`** (AC Superposition Validation):
   - Previously reported `VerifiedBad` because AC rewrite steps could not be validated.
   - The kernel now properly identifies incomplete replay steps and fails closed as
     **`Unknown`** rather than claiming an invalid derivation.
