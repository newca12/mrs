## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20, 2026, a full standalone CASC-30 UEQ (Unit Equality) campaign
was evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints:

- **300 problems evaluated** at the official CASC-30 wall-clock limit of **240 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Total campaign wall clock time was 48,578 s
  (~13.49 hours).
- **124 refutations generated (`Unsatisfiable`)**: 41.33% raw solve rate. The
  remaining outcomes were 167 `Timeout` (55.67%) and 9 `GaveUp` (3.00%), with
  zero process errors or crashes.
- **Strict self-certification: 120 of 124 `VerifiedGood` (96.77%)**, with **0
  `VerifiedBad` (0.0% unsoundness)**, 4 `Unknown`, 0 verifier timeouts, and 0
  withheld proofs.
- **Soundness resolution of the 2026-10-16 failures**: The previous CASC-mode run
  (`campaign-casc30-ueq-feq-W8CASCHJ1-20261016`) had 5 proofs rejected as
  `VerifiedBad` and 1 refutation without a proof root. In this campaign, **all
  soundness issues are fully resolved**:
  - `MVA008-1` and `MVA009-1` (axiom leaf matching): both strictly **`VerifiedGood`**.
  - `REL034-2` (symbol arity conflict in proof): strictly **`VerifiedGood`**.
  - `REL045-2` (missing refutation root): strictly **`VerifiedGood`**.
  - `RNG009-5` and `RNG009-7` (incomplete AC rewriting): correctly fail closed as
    **`Unknown`**, rather than `VerifiedBad`.
- **Residual gaps**: Exactly 4 proofs return `Unknown` (`GRP179-2`, `REL027-3`,
  `RNG009-5`, `RNG009-7`), all due to incomplete AC superposition rewrite replay.

## 4. Detailed Solved Problem Telemetry & Key Observations

### 4.1 Resolution of Prior `VerifiedBad` Deficiencies

In the previous CASC-mode campaign (`campaign-casc30-ueq-feq-W8CASCHJ1-20261016`),
five proofs failed strict replay with `VerifiedBad` and one had a malformed root.
In this campaign:

1. **`MVA008-1` & `MVA009-1`** (Axiom Leaf Matching):
   - Previously failed because cited axiom leaves did not match `residual_b` /
     `residual_c` under syntactic leaf checking.
   - Now certify strictly as **`VerifiedGood`** (108.5 s and 27.0 s wall time).
2. **`REL034-2`** (Symbol Arity Inconsistency):
   - Previously rejected due to signature conflicts across clauses.
   - Now certifies strictly as **`VerifiedGood`** (206.0 s wall time).
3. **`REL045-2`** (Missing Proof Root):
   - Previously generated a refutation but omitted the `$false` root node.
   - Now emits a complete derivation and certifies as **`VerifiedGood`** (9.7 s).
4. **`RNG009-5` & `RNG009-7`** (AC Superposition Validation):
   - Previously reported `VerifiedBad` because AC rewrite steps could not be validated.
   - The kernel now properly identifies incomplete replay steps and fails closed as
     **`Unknown`** rather than claiming an invalid derivation.
