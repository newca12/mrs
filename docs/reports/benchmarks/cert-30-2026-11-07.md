# CASC-30 complete all-division campaign (2026-11-07–10)

> The archived run contains a result row for all **1,101 scheduled CASC-30
> problems**, including all 101 ICU rows. It produced 365 raw decisive results;
> the post-hoc strict audit certifies **319 refutations + 43 EPS models = 362**,
> with 0 `VerifiedBad` proofs and 0 invalid models. This is a complete campaign
> archive, but **not a canonical eight-core CASC measurement or an official
> score**: the dirty producer ran on a 16-physical-core host with only 108,816 MB
> reported effective memory against the 128-GiB allowance. One ICU process was
> killed by `SIGKILL`; its cause is unconfirmed.

## Completion and raw outcomes

The campaign began at `20261107_234040` and `run.log` ends with the normal
`[casc] Done` line after `1101/1101 completed`. The run took place from November
7 to November 10; the sum of `actual_wall_time_s` across rows is **192,451.9 s
(53.46 hours)** with sequential `jobs=1`. All six CASC-30 divisions used their
official time limits: 240 s for FNE/FEQ/UEQ, 120 s for EPU/EPS, and 480 s for
ICU.

The audit recorded **no `VerifiedBad` proofs, invalid models, or strict-checker
timeouts**. Its three non-certified raw answers are:

- `FEQ/LCL894+1`: `Unknown`; AC-equivalence replay could not establish the
  required normalization within the checker’s implemented search.
- `FEQ/GEO559+1`: the prover found a refutation but withheld its 68,510,222-byte
  proof (160,280 nodes), above the 33,554,432-byte proof limit.
- `EPS/SYN720-1`: model checking was inconclusive because the model input has
  unresolved `%include` files. This is not an invalid-model finding.

The sole search `Error` is `ICU/CSI008+1`. The row records exit 137 / signal 9,
`SIGKILL: cause unconfirmed`, after 103.625 s, with 125,269 MB peak RSS. The raw
stderr says the problem has 2,004,866 axioms and reports
`mem_budget_mb=131072`, `effective_mem_mb=108816`, and
`mem_unrepresentable=1`. The campaign continued and saved rows for the remaining
ICU problems. **Do not label this particular kill an OOM:** the run artifacts do
not establish why the process received `SIGKILL`. The highest recorded virtual
memory was 134,967 MB on `ICU/EEE009+1`.

## Interpretation

This is the first of the October/November all-division attempts whose saved
`run.csv` covers all 1,101 scheduled problems; the October 30 and November 3
archives stop after 1,032 rows. Its 362 strictly certified outcomes are a
verified result for this archive and audit identity, **not an official score or
a standardized CASC baseline**. `MRS_HARDWARE=casc` fixed eight workers but did
not pin them to eight physical cores; the host had 16 physical cores and less
effective memory than the configured 128-GiB allowance. Use paired, matched
campaigns before drawing a schedule or source-revision conclusion.
