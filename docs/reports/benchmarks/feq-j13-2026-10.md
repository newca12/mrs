## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20, 2026, a full standalone CASC-J13 FEQ (First-Order with Equality)
campaign was evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints:

- **300 problems evaluated** at the official CASC-J13 wall-clock limit of **180 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Total campaign wall clock time was 42,773 s
  (~11.88 hours).
- **68 refutations generated (`Theorem`)**: 22.67% raw solve rate. The remaining
  outcomes were 193 `Timeout` (64.33%) and 39 `GaveUp` (13.00%), with zero process
  errors or crashes.
- **Strict self-certification: 68 of 68 `VerifiedGood` (100.0%)**, with **0
  `VerifiedBad` (0.0% unsoundness)**, **0 `Unknown`**, **0 checker timeouts**,
  and **0 omitted proofs**.
- **Flawless Proof Verification**: Every single generated refutation passed
  strict proof-kernel verification without exception.
- **Rapid Solves**: Median solve time across all theorems was **1.860 s**. Nearly
  half (32 of 68, 47.1%) solved in under 1 second, and 41 of 68 (60.3%) solved in
  under 5 seconds.
- **Large Proof Certification**: Three proofs exceeded 8 MB—`MGT005+1` (8.3 MB),
  `SEV677+1` (16.9 MB), and `MGT079+1` (23.1 MB, 137k proof nodes)—and all three
  certified cleanly as `VerifiedGood` in 1.4 s, 1.2 s, and 2.9 s verifier time.
