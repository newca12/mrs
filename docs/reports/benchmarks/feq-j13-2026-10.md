## 1. Executive Summary

On October 20 and October 21, 2026, two full standalone CASC-J13 FEQ (First-Order with Equality)
campaigns were evaluated and audited under canonical CASC hardware constraints on `mtsdev03`:

- **300 problems evaluated** per campaign at the official CASC-J13 wall-clock limit of **180 s**
  per problem, running with `MRS_HARDWARE=casc`, 8 parallel workers, and `jobs=1`
  (sequential problem processing). Campaign wall clock times were 42,773 s (~11.88 hours)
  on Oct 20 and 42,708 s (~11.86 hours) on Oct 21.
- **68 refutations generated on Oct 20 (22.67%) and 69 on Oct 21 (23.00%)**:
  All 68 theorems solved in Oct 20 re-solved in Oct 21. In addition, `SWV406+1` (which timed out at
  178.8 s on Oct 20) solved in 162.8 s on Oct 21.
- **Strict self-certification**:
  - Oct 20: **68 of 68 `VerifiedGood` (100.0%)**, with **0 `VerifiedBad`**, **0 `Unknown`**,
    0 checker timeouts, and 0 omitted proofs.
  - Oct 21: **68 of 69 `VerifiedGood` (98.55%)**, with **0 `VerifiedBad`**, **0 `Unknown`**,
    0 checker timeouts, and 1 `Proof Omitted` (`SWV406+1`).
  - Across all 136 emitted proofs across both runs, **100% of emitted proofs certified strictly
    as `VerifiedGood` with zero unsoundness or replay gaps**.
- **Proof size ceiling on `SWV406+1`**: `SWV406+1` derived a massive refutation (319,894 nodes,
  83.4 MB proof text) and withheld proof emission (`proof_emitted=false`) because it exceeded
  the default 32 MB (`--proof-bytes-limit`) ceiling.
- **Rapid Solves**: Median solve time across all theorems was **1.860 s** on Oct 20 and
  **1.356 s** on Oct 21. Over 46% solved in under 1 second, and over 60% solved in under 5 seconds.
- **Large Proof Certification**: Three proofs in Oct 20 exceeded 8 MB—`MGT005+1` (8.3 MB),
  `SEV677+1` (16.9 MB), and `MGT079+1` (23.1 MB, 137k proof nodes)—and all three
  certified cleanly as `VerifiedGood` in under 3 seconds verifier time.
