## Results

The campaign evaluated the complete 800-problem CASC-J13 corpus across FEQ, FNE,
and UEQ. The archived `run.csv` reports:

Every process completed with exit code 0; no `Error` or `ResourceOut` was
recorded. Summed actual solver wall-clock time (`actual_wall_time_s`) was
106,726.7 seconds (29.65 hours, `jobs=1`). Solve times for refutations:

- **FEQ (70 solved):** mean 17.46 s, median 1.73 s (min 0.15 s, max 151.42 s)

The maximum recorded virtual-address-space peaks (`peak_virtual_mb`) were
89,262 MB on FNE (`NLP261+1`), 75,511 MB on FEQ (`CSR066+5`), and 77,830 MB on
UEQ (`GRP181-1`); these are not resident-memory measurements. The maximum

FEQ (`CSR036+5`), and 47,664 MB on UEQ (`RNG021-6`). All recorded RSS peaks
were below the host's 95,979 MB RAM, with zero resource-out terminations.

### 2. Full Elimination of Empty Proof Envelopes (UI-11)
In the October 28 run (`cert-j13-20261028-093240`), a stale pre-`c619394` binary
caused 40 empty proof envelopes. In this October 30 campaign, **`no_proof` count is 0**.
All 33 formerly empty-envelope problems that solved in this campaign emitted complete,
valid TSTP derivations that certified strictly as `VerifiedGood`.

### 3. Proof-Extraction Validation for `REL045-2` (UI-12)
In the October 28 run, `REL045-2` emitted only a header with `proof_nodes=0`. In this
campaign, `REL045-2` derived a complete 25,821-byte proof with 106 nodes and certified
strictly as `VerifiedGood`, confirming the resolution of UI-12 under live campaign
conditions.

### 4. Resolution of AC Superposition Unknowns (UI-1)
In the October 20/21 campaigns, `RNG009-5` and `RNG009-7` returned `Unknown` during
strict audit due to incomplete AC rewrite replay. In this campaign:
- **`RNG009-5`** and **`RNG009-7`** certified natively as **`VerifiedGood`**.
- **`LAT142-1`** (node `c26184`), the single initial `Unknown` in the archive, failed
  because `rigid_match_term` required identical flattened operand lengths when matching
  AC terms, rejecting absorption of multiple target operands by an unbound pattern variable.
  Extending AC operand matching in `mrs-proof-kernel` to permit variable operand absorption
  resolves `c26184` and certifies the entire 323-node proof as **`VerifiedGood`**.
